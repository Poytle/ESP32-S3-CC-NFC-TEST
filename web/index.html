Signing support
The reader sends INTERNAL AUTHENTICATE after inspection when the application advertises DDA and its DDOL is supported (9F3704, or the terminal default when absent). Results include the random challenge, APDU status, and returned signature bytes. Unsupported cards, rejected commands, and transport failures are shown explicitly. A signature already returned during GPO is also displayed.
This prototype does not verify real EMV RSA certificates or signatures, and does not perform CDA or issuer authorization. It sends no GENERATE AC, PIN attempts, or payment authorization. GET PROCESSING OPTIONS and signing requests may update card state. Public card data and full PANs are visible to clients connected to this open local network.
